Privacy Policy – Win Auto Inc.

Win Auto Inc. ("Win Auto", "we") operates the website winautoinc.com and an internal tool called "Win Auto Garage" that we use to prepare vehicle listings for our own inventory.

Information we collect

  • Through our website: information you choose to send us (for example your name, phone number or email when you contact us), and standard website analytics.
  • Through Win Auto Garage: the tool accesses only Win Auto's own company Google Drive account, to read the photos and videos of vehicles we are selling and to save edited photos and listing text back to that account. It does not access any customer's Google account.

How we use information

We use it to respond to inquiries, to sell and advertise our vehicles, and to run our business. Win Auto Garage uses AI services (Anthropic's Claude) to help edit vehicle photos and write listing text from our own vehicle information.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share information only with service providers that help us operate (such as hosting, Google and AI services), or when required by law.

Google user data

Win Auto Garage's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google Drive data is used only to provide the tool's features to Win Auto and is not transferred to others except as needed to provide those features, to comply with law, or with our consent.
